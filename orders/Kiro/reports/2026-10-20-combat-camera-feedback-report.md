## Work completed

Kept small, as the chief asked: one module, its tests, a parameter guide, an event contract for Aki, one local export check.

- **Module** `scripts/camera/camera_impact.gd` (`class_name CameraImpact`): `light_hit(dir)`, `heavy_hit(dir)`, `impulse(dir, peak_px, duration_ms)`. It writes `Camera2D.offset` only and ends at exactly `Vector2.ZERO`.
- **Design (5 lines).** (1) Envelope: a pure function of the time since each impulse started: linear rise to EXACTLY the peak in one 60 Hz tick (16.7 ms), then quadratic ease-out `(1-u)^2` to EXACTLY 0 at the duration. (2) Stacking: unit direction x magnitude per impulse, combined as the magnitude-weighted mean direction x root-sum-square of the magnitudes (same-direction hits are sub-linear, opposite hits cancel), hard cap `MAX_TOTAL_PX` 6, at most 8 live impulses. (3) Runtime: a lazy helper node under the player's `Camera2D` (found by the `player` group), frame time with `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS` so local hitstop and pause do not freeze it, screen px divided by the CURRENT zoom. (4) Edge clamp (needed, measured): Godot 4.7.2 adds `Camera2D.offset` after smoothing AND after the limit clamp, so the module keeps the view inside the camera limits itself; a kick toward a pinned wall shrinks to the room left. The clamp runs twice per frame: before the write, and again from the centre the engine reports AFTER the write, because `Camera2D.set_offset()` makes the engine advance the position smoothing one extra step for the rendered frame (review fix, see Issues). (5) Lab keys, `?camera_impact=`, telemetry; no zoom pulse.
- **Tunables and numbers** (PROVISIONAL, order bands in brackets): light 2.5 screen px / 80 ms (2-3 px, 60-100 ms), heavy 5.0 px / 120 ms (4-6 px, 100-140 ms), cap 6.0 px, rise 1/60 s, 8 live impulses, longest 5000 ms, reduced = x0.5, slow demo 1500 ms. The full table with "punchier / calmer" columns is in `docs/development/CAMERA_IMPACT.md`.
- **Settings:** `CameraImpact.settings.enabled` / `.scale` (0..1; 0.5 = reduced; 0 = off); page URL `?camera_impact=off|reduced|on` parsed once in `WebBridge` like `?camera=` (first value wins, garbage = on); default `reduced` when the browser reports `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (the URL still wins).
- **Demo trigger (my files only):** lab keys `7` light, `8` heavy, `9` burst (three lights 40 ms apart), `0` slow heavy (5 px peak stretched to 1500 ms), active only while the lab is open, plus a "Cam kick  7 8 9 0" button row. They kick along the player's facing.
- **Telemetry** in `__SHINOBI_STATE__` (additive; screen px): `cfx_enabled`, `cfx_scale`, `cfx_active`, `cfx_offset_x`, `cfx_offset_y`, `cfx_peak_px`, `cfx_count`.
- **Docs:** `docs/development/CAMERA_IMPACT.md` (parameter guide, tuning procedure, edge behaviour including the clamp from the centre after the write and the engine quirk of the extra smoothing step on a moving camera, trace table, the event contract for Aki with a paste-ready snippet, a line for Orcha/Codex), a bullet in the camera section of `GODOT_PROJECT_HANDOFF.md`, one line in `TESTING.md`.

## Findings about other agents' work and hazards
- **Single writer of `Camera2D.offset`.** The module writes exactly zero when an effect ends, so a second writer (for example Orcha's future camera director) would be erased. Documented in the guide and the handoff; the director should send impulses through `CameraImpact` or agree on one combined writer with Kiro first.
- **Aki's integration is a contract only.** One call per confirmed contact next to `enemy.take_damage(...)`; the snippet was proved to compile and to leave Aki's whole suite green (722 passed) on a throwaway copy of the final tree at that time (not repeated after the review fix; the contract did not change). With today's attack table only `light3` (75 ms) counts as heavy under the suggested `hitstop_ms >= 70` rule.
- **Hitstop** is a local per-entity timer, so the effect keeps ticking during it (VERIFIED in-engine: "real frames during local hitstop").
- **An engine quirk remains while an effect runs on a MOVING camera (documented, the clamp cannot remove it).** The extra smoothing step above also means the rendered frame is ahead of the no-kick camera path by up to one frame of camera travel for as long as the effect runs. The independent review measured at most 5.69 world px walking and 8.78 sprinting (wide 2.5 and 3.9 screen px, close 6.9 and 10.6 screen px), constant during the effect and gone the frame after it ends; the persistent smoothing state is restored by the engine, so nothing drifts (deviation from the no-kick path on the last frame after the effect: 0.000000). The persistent state is unchanged; the rendered frames during the effect are not. Expected feel (nobody has felt it yet): when a hit lands while the player is running, the picture hops FORWARD in the direction of travel by one frame of camera travel (5.69 world px walking, 8.78 sprinting = 10.6 screen px at the close zoom, about twice the 5 px kick itself) and hops BACK by the same amount when the effect ends, on top of the kick; it is more than a kick that reads a bit larger. "Hit while sprinting at the close zoom" is on the first-playtest checklist (guide, tuning procedure step 3; question 7 below). These figures are the reviewer's headless measurements at a fixed 60 FPS; I did not re-measure them. A future camera director that writes `Camera2D.offset`, or that reads `get_screen_center_position()` while a kick runs, inherits the quirk. Possible later improvement, not done: run the helper before the camera (a lower `process_priority` than the camera's) and use the centre after the write as the exact prediction of the camera's own update, which removes the extra step. Described in `docs/development/CAMERA_IMPACT.md`, "Edge and limit behaviour".
