## Silhouette and identity
- Original young Black male shinobi, athletic/muscular, confident serious face.
- Black medium-length dreadlocks gathered into a high tied-back style; a few locks frame forehead/temples.
- Unmasked and face fully visible in front and side views; no head-covering mask.
- Neutral upright turnaround with consistent height, limb proportions, and outfit geometry in front, right side profile, and back views.

## Default outfit (baseline, not equipment upgrade)
- Dark olive-gray armored chest vest, angular chest panel, dark webbing, metallic buckles, layered black tactical harness.
- Black sleeveless high-collar base layer, dark neck wrap/collar, exposed muscular shoulders/upper arms.
- Black fingerless gloves, black protective forearm pieces and white cloth wraps on forearms.
- Multiple dark belts, utility straps, hip pouches, and black loose tapered cargo-style shinobi trousers.
- White wraps around calves/shins; chunky black open-toe tactical shinobi sandals.
- Mostly black/charcoal palette with muted olive armor and bright white wraps. No decorative cape fabric.

## Explicit exclusions
- **NO mask, NO cape/cloak, NO sword, NO scabbard, NO sword straps, NO carried weapon** in the default character.
- Do not reintroduce these from earlier concept sheets. Weapons and equipment may be considered later as optional upgrades, not the baseline.
