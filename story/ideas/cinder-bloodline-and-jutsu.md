# SHINOBI RUSH — Cinder Bloodline & Jutsu Idea Bank

> **Status: STORY IDEAS / NOT CANON.** Preserve these concepts for future selection and playtesting. Existing canon is in [The Cinders Legacy](../lore/the-cinders-legacy.md). In particular: the player is 12, begins with **no ninjutsu**, the village was destroyed **12 years ago** during a war, and the great-grandfather's mysterious power is **still officially unnamed**. Do not silently rewrite those facts or implement these powers as unlocked starting abilities.

## Core possibility: Fire + Earth → Lava Release
- **Candidate Kekkei Genkai:** Lava Release (Yōton), combining Fire Release (Katon) and Earth Release (Doton).
- The Fifth and last Hokage, the protagonist's great-grandfather, might have mastered it; the scrolls in the Ashen Ruins could preserve its techniques.
- Possible village distinction: everyday shinobi trained in ash/cinder techniques; the ruling family carried a rare Lava Release bloodline.
- **Awakening concept:** the protagonist visits the destroyed village and begins discovering this inherited power through story milestones. This is not yet decided canon.
- **Visual arc:** bright liquid magma → hardened black basalt with glowing orange fissures → large volcanic manifestations.
- **Unresolved:** how the bloodline passes through the family; what caused the war and destruction; whether volcanoes were deliberately triggered. Current canon says ninja destroyed the village with large area attacks, not that volcanoes erupted.

## Naruto reference: Rōshi (inspiration, not game canon)
- Rōshi: Hidden Stone jinchūriki of Son Gokū, the Four-Tails; Fire + Earth chakra; Lava Release.
- **Scorching Stream Rock Technique:** multiple molten rock projectiles.
- **Lava Release Chakra Mode:** coats the body with damaging molten chakra.
- **Scorching Armoured Fist:** heated close-range strikes.
- **Flower–Fruit Mountain:** major volcanic eruption associated with the Four-Tails' power.
- **Important distinction:** Rōshi's tailed-beast transformations are not inherently available to the protagonist. We can adapt the volcanic *combat fantasy* without a tailed beast.
- Other Naruto Lava Release expressions: Mei's corrosive form, Dodai's rubber-like form, Kurotsuchi's quicklime form. Our candidate identity is primarily molten rock/basalt.

## Fire Release (Katon) — candidate learnable ninjutsu
- **Great Fireball Technique:** charged ranged fireball; larger blast when held.
- **Phoenix Sage Fire Technique:** three to five small spreading fire projectiles.
- **Dragon Flame Technique:** narrow, concentrated flame attack; Naruto inspiration traditionally uses a wire/cord.
- **Great Fire Annihilation:** late-game advancing wall of fire.

## Earth Release (Doton) — candidate learnable ninjutsu
- **Earth-Style Wall:** temporary solid cover; blocks kunai/projectiles and movement.
- **Hiding Like a Mole:** short underground reposition, resurfacing near an enemy.
- **Double Suicide Decapitation:** short-range underground grab/root.
- **Earth Flow River:** flowing mud hazard that slows/displaces foes.
- **Fist Rock Technique:** rock-coated melee strike, knockback and guard break.

## Ash-based techniques
**Canon Naruto reference, not a separate canonical Ash Release bloodline:**
- **Fire Release: Ash Pile Burning** (Asuma): combustible ash cloud, ignited for explosion.
- **Fire Release: Hiding in Ash and Dust** (Madara): ash obscures visibility and burns.

**Original SHINOBI RUSH proposal: Cinder Style**, a regional school/tradition of ash techniques. It is **not** a confirmed third chakra nature or canonical Naruto Kekkei Genkai.

## Combat combinations and progression
- **Cinder Cloud → Burning Ash:** beginner two-step trap/combo, potentially first learned elemental chain **after** story unlock.
- **Earth Wall → Great Fireball:** defend then punish.
- **Hiding Like a Mole → Fist Rock:** subterranean ambush.
- **Earth Flow River → Phoenix Sage Fire:** slow and bombard.
- **Earth Wall → Lava Release:** late-game molten defensive barrier.
- Potential mastery tracks: Fire proficiency, Earth proficiency, later Lava bloodline awakening.
- Combat role: close-to-mid-range battlefield controller, not only a fireball caster.
