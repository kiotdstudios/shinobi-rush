## Official Village Identity (Canon)
- A small but thriving shinobi village surrounded by dormant volcanoes.
- Crest: concept **#3**, the minimalist circular swirling flame, on headbands.
- Destroyed in a war. Most residents died; survivors scattered.
- Final leader: the **Fifth and last Hokage**, the player's great-grandfather.

## The Player's Family (Canon, tidied)
- You are the **great-grandson of the Fifth Hokage**.
- Your **parents died in the war**.
- Your **sister** is your last known living family member.
- An **old man from the village** took you both away before the war reached village grounds. Your parents paid him to look after you.
- He **died when you turned 10**, having taught you only the basics.
- Before he died he hinted at **a power your great-grandfather mastered**, which is written in **scrolls in the Ashen Ruins**. Nobody since has mastered it.
- Fix applied: the original text said both "grandfather" and "great-grandfather". This version uses **great-grandfather only**. If a grandfather should exist (and what became of him), say so and I will add him.

## The Opening (Canon)
- The old man used to bring you to the **Ancient Shrine** on the outskirts of **Blightwood Forest**.
- A **monument to the villagers who fell in the war** stands there. Your great-grandfather ordered it built as one of his last acts.
- You visit it to pay your respects, then **begin your journey through Blightwood toward the Ashen Ruins**, as a genin starting out.

## Story Prose
Twelve years ago a war swallowed a small village at the foot of sleeping volcanoes. Its people died or scattered, and its Fifth Hokage fell with it.

Two children were already gone. Their parents had paid an old man to carry them away before the fire reached the gates. He raised them quietly, taught them what he could, and told them about the place they came from. When the boy turned ten, the old man died.

Two years later the boy stands at the Ancient Shrine where the old man used to bring them, in front of the monument his great-grandfather ordered raised. He isn't looking for a throne. He wants to see where he came from, and to find the scrolls the old man spoke of.

## Open Questions (owner to decide)
These are not canon until answered.
1. **Sister:** where is she when you leave, and is she safe? Does she stay, follow later, or turn up again?
2. **Trigger:** why leave now, two years after the old man's death? A deathbed promise, a map, a scroll fragment?
3. **Genin rank:** who granted it, since the village is gone? Maybe the old man made the headband himself.
4. **The old man:** name, face and relation to the village (guard, clan member, friend of your parents).
5. **The power:** what is it, and how does it tie to Chidori and substitution? (Suggestion: keep it unnamed in the opening.)
6. **Who destroyed the village:** left unresolved on purpose, but an early hint or a rival after the same scrolls would help.
7. **Monument:** are names carved on it, and are your parents' among them?
8. **Tone of the opening:** grief, wonder, or quiet determination.
9. **Why the Level 1 bandits are in Blightwood:** scavengers picking at the abandoned road to the ruins?

## Proposed Structure (Not Yet Canon)
- **Prologue:** the shrine visit and the monument. Tutorial: movement, dodge, basic attacks.
- **Chapter 1 — Blightwood Forest:** exploration and early combat (the Level 1 bandits).
- **Chapter 2 — The Ashen Ruins:** the destroyed village, and the scrolls.

## Proposed Long-Term Threads (Not Yet Canon)
1. The Fifth Hokage's legacy and the hard choices he made in the war.
2. The scattered survivors, some welcoming and some resentful.
3. What becoming Hokage means: protecting people and giving them somewhere to belong.
4. Central mystery: why was a village this small destroyed so completely?

## Tone and Guardrails
- Start as a personal pilgrimage, not a save-the-world prophecy.
- The Hokage ambition grows during the journey; it is not there at the start.
- Keep the sister emotionally important.
- Keep the cause of the war unresolved until deliberately developed.
- Do not treat proposed beats as confirmed without owner approval.
