## Official Village Identity (Canon)
- **Name:** Village Hidden in the Cinders (Haigakure).
- **Official crest:** Concept **#3**, the minimalist circular swirling flame emblem designed for shinobi headbands.
- **Setting:** Once a small but thriving shinobi village surrounded by dormant volcanoes.
- **Fate:** Destroyed **80 years before the game's present** during a war.
- **Aftermath:** Most residents died. Survivors scattered to other areas of the ninja world.
- **Final leader:** The village's **Fifth and last Hokage**. (Title is part of the user's current lore; terminology can be revisited later if desired.)

## Protagonist and Motivation (Canon)
- The player is the **great-grandson of the Fifth Hokage**.
- The player's **sister is his last known living family member**.
- The player leaves his sister and travels back to the destroyed village to pay respects to his late great-grandfather and his legacy.
- This pilgrimage begins a much larger journey that eventually inspires the player to become **Hokage of a village someday**.
- The player does **not need to start the game already determined to become Hokage**. His ambition can develop as the story unfolds (narrative interpretation of the stated arc).

## The Village That Disappeared (Story Prose)
Eighty years ago, the Village Hidden in the Cinders was a small but thriving shinobi settlement surrounded by dormant volcanoes. Though it lacked the size and influence of the great ninja villages, its people had built a proud civilization with their own traditions, clans, and way of life.

Then came the war.

The village was devastated. Most of its inhabitants were killed, and the few who survived scattered across the ninja world. With no people left to lead and no village left to protect, the reign of its Fifth Hokage became its last.

Haigakure faded from the maps, surviving only in stories and the memories of its descendants.

## Eighty Years Later (Story Prose)
The player is the great-grandson of Haigakure's Fifth and final Hokage.

He has grown up far from the land of his ancestors. His sister is his last known living family member, and together they represent what little remains of their bloodline.

But the weight of his family's history has always followed him.

One day, he decides to leave his sister behind and journey to the ruins of the Village Hidden in the Cinders. His purpose is simple: visit the resting place of his great-grandfather, pay his respects, and understand the legacy he inherited.

He isn't traveling to become a hero.

He isn't trying to reclaim a throne.

He just wants to know where he came from.

But what begins as a pilgrimage eventually becomes something much greater.

**His journey will awaken a dream of becoming the Hokage of a village someday.**

## Proposed Opening Game Structure (Not Yet Canon)

### Prologue — The Last Family
A brief scene between the player and his sister before he leaves home. Establish their relationship and why the pilgrimage matters.

### Chapter 1 — Blightwood Forest
The player enters the forest leading toward Haigakure. The **Ancient Shrine** is the first playable location, introducing movement, exploration, and early combat. This fits the current Ancient Shrine starting-area art and level-design work.

### Chapter 2 — The Ashen Ruins
The player reaches the destroyed village and begins discovering what happened during the war and what his great-grandfather left behind.

## Proposed Long-Term Story Threads (Not Yet Canon)

1. **The Fifth Hokage's legacy:** The player knows his great-grandfather as a respected leader, but gradually discovers the difficult choices he made during the war.
2. **The scattered survivors:** Descendants of Haigakure's surviving clans live throughout the ninja world. Some welcome the player; others resent his family.
3. **The meaning of becoming Hokage:** The player initially equates leadership with strength and recognition, then learns it is about protecting people and giving them somewhere to belong.
4. **Central mystery:** *Why was such a small village destroyed so completely during the war?* This can anchor the main storyline.

## Tone and Narrative Guardrails
- Start with a **personal pilgrimage**, not an immediate save-the-world prophecy.
- Let the Hokage ambition **develop organically** through encounters and discoveries.
- Preserve the emotional significance of the sister as the last known family member.
- Keep the Fifth Hokage's actions and the exact cause of the village's destruction **unresolved until intentionally developed**.
- Avoid treating proposed mysteries or chapter beats as confirmed facts without user approval.
