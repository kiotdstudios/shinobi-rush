# SHINOBI RUSH — THE CINDERS LEGACY

> Story bible • **The Village Hidden in the Cinders** (English name only; no romanized "-gakure" name is used).
>
> **Canon** = decided by the owner. **Timeline/Resolved** = derived or chosen to make the canon consistent ("use whatever makes sense"). **Proposed** = ideas to approve later. **Open** = still to decide.

## Timeline

Present day = Year 0. The player is **12**.

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| About 100 years ago | The village is founded among dormant volcanoes. Five Hokage in about a century. |

| 12 years ago | Other ninja destroy the village with large area attacks. Most residents die, including both parents. The Fifth Hokage (the player's great-grandfather) dies in the war. ## The Village (Canon)
- **The Village Hidden in the Cinders.** A small but thriving shinobi village surrounded by dormant volcanoes.
- Crest: concept **#3**, the minimalist circular swirling flame, on headbands.
- Destroyed in a war by other ninja using large area attacks. Most residents died; survivors scattered. Nobody rebuilt it.
- The surviving ninja world has mostly forgotten it; the crest is rarely seen because almost everyone who wore it died.
- Final leader: the **Fifth and last Hokage**.

## Family (Canon + Resolved)
- **Great-grandfather:** the Fifth Hokage. He mastered a power that nobody since has mastered; it is written in scrolls in the Ashen Ruins. (Resolved: "grandfather" in earlier notes now means him. There is no separate grandfather.)
- **Parents:** died in the war.
- **Sister:** about 5 years older (about 17 now). The war left her hating that ninja must fight and kill. She chose **the healing side of the ninja world** and studies medical ninja arts. She lives in a **hamlet**; **as the player progresses, the hamlet grows into the new village**. She stays behind when he leaves. Her name is open.
- **The player:** a determined 12-year-old, no name yet. He wants to become **Hokage**. All he knows of his people comes from the old man's stories and from random ninja who passed through the hamlet.

## The Old Man (Resolved, proposed backstory)
Owner: "one of his friends, you can come up with a name and backstory."
- **Name (proposal): Genzō.**
- A former jonin of the village and a close friend of the Fifth Hokage, who served as his bodyguard in the last years.
- The Fifth gave him a last order: take the children away before the war reached the village. He obeyed and lived with the weight of surviving when his village and friend did not.
- He kept the Fifth's old **headband** for the player. He never told the children everything. He hinted at the power in the scrolls but never said what it was.
- Appearance (proposal): grey-bearded, bad knee, a burn scar along the forearm, walks with a staff he sometimes uses as a training pole.
- He used to take the children to the Ancient Shrine on the edge of Blightwood to see the memorial.
- Two short flashbacks of training with him (proposal): water-walking practice on the hamlet's pond, and the night he told them about the volcanoes.

## Starting Skills (Canon)
The old man taught only the basics:
- Taijutsu, kunai throwing, substitution, and **chakra control (running on water)**.
- Knowledge of paper tags and bombs, healing and chakra gourds, and similar kit.
- **No ninjutsu.** The player trains his body every day and pushes himself to be stronger.
- Game mapping: kunai, substitution, paper tag (throwable bomb) and gourds exist already. Chakra-control water walking is not yet a mechanic (idea: lets the player cross some water). The Chidori-style lightning in the game is a **test jutsu, not canon**. It can be reworked or removed later.

## Genin Rank (Canon)
- The village is gone, but the hamlet has a small group of ninja and a **small academy**, which is where the player trained and earned his rank.
- Before the player sets out, the old man gives him the Fifth Hokage's old headband with the village crest.
- Later, people who see the crest react: "you're from there? We never see anyone with that crest; they all died."

## The Power (Open)
Undecided, so it stays unnamed. In the opening it is only ever "the power in the scrolls of the Ashen Ruins". The Chidori test jutsu is not tied to it.

## The Opening (Canon)
1. **The Ancient Shrine** at the edge of Blightwood: the old man used to bring the children here.
2. **The memorial:** built on the Fifth's last orders, for the **whole village**, dedicated to the fallen. It lists the council members, known doctors, skilled tradesmen and other important figures. (Open: whether the parents' names appear.)
3. The player pays his respects and sets out through Blightwood Forest toward the Ashen Ruins.
4. **Tutorial** runs through Blightwood against the Level 1 bandits.

## Tone (Canon)
All three at once: **grief** (the old man's death, the memorial), **wonder** (what is there to find, what history must I uncover) and **determination** (he wants to be Hokage). Suggested mapping: grief at the shrine, wonder on the road and in the ruins, determination in the training and the fights.
(Note: the earlier guardrail "the Hokage ambition grows later" is replaced. He wants to be Hokage from the start; what it means is what grows.)

## World Principle: Enemies Live In The World (Canon)
Enemies are not parked in one place. Like real people they have objectives and move around. Bandits want money, so they search low-population areas where they are unlikely to be noticed. No special reason is needed for them to be in Blightwood.
- Implication for the game: the Level 1 Bandit currently guards a home spot and roams briefly. Giving bandits routes and goals (search an area, move on, stash loot) is a future AI task.

## Proposed Structure (Not Yet Canon)
- **Prologue:** the hamlet and a short scene with the sister; flashback of the old man's last day.
- **Chapter 1: Blightwood Forest.** Shrine, memorial, early combat.
- **Chapter 2: The Ashen Ruins.** The destroyed village, the scrolls.
- **Hamlet growth:** the hamlet visibly grows into a new village as the player advances (the sister's side of the story).

## Proposed Long-Term Threads (Not Yet Canon)
1. The Fifth Hokage's legacy and the hard choices he made in the war.
2. The scattered survivors, some welcoming and some resentful.
3. The player's idea of Hokage (strength) against the sister's (protecting people).
4. Central mystery: why was such a small village destroyed so completely?

## Visual / Level Design
- Crest #3: circular swirling flame.
- Blightwood Forest: gray slate isometric terrain, lavender-blossom trees, muted blue-green foliage.
- Ashen Ruins: destroyed stone settlement, crumbling walls, ash-gray masonry, muted red banners, ember-lit structures. After 12 years it should still look recently ruined.
- Ancient Shrine: starting area (now in the default arena). The memorial with its name list is a natural prop to add.
